One sentence, at most 200 characters, explaining the modern internet to a
traveler from 1800 — built only from things they already know: a road, a
post house, a library.

> It is a road of wire joining every house on earth to one great post house
> and library, where a letter you send arrives in a blink and any book you
> seek lies open upon your table.

Run it: `node main.ts` — it writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it.
